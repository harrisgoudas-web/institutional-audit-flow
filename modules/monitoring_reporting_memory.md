# Monitoring, Reporting & Institutional Memory Framework

## Purpose
Define continuous monitoring, reporting obligations, and institutional memory mechanisms across the full accountability lifecycle (A1–A4). This module ensures transparency, accountability, and effective remedies by capturing, reviewing, and disclosing decision activity, escalation outcomes, and remedial actions.

## Legal Basis
- **ICCPR** Articles 2(3), 14, and 19 (effective remedy, fair hearing, access to information).
- **ECHR** Articles 6, 10, and 13 (fair trial, freedom of expression/information, effective remedy).
- **Principles:** transparency, accountability, and effective remedy.

## Scope
- **Coverage:** All decisions, escalations, appeals, remedies, and external referrals generated under the Codex framework.
- **Lifecycle Stages:** A1 (intake/initial decision), A2 (escalation), A3 (appeal/remedy), A4 (external referral/enforcement).
- **Actors:** decision-making units, escalation handlers, appeals panels, compliance and audit functions, and external oversight interfaces.

## Monitoring Indicators & Metrics
- **Timeliness:** response SLAs met, escalation time-to-acknowledge, appeal resolution time.
- **Outcome Quality:** reversal/correction rates, remedy completeness, compliance with reason-giving standards.
- **Access & Fairness:** access-to-records fulfillment rates, non-retaliation incidents, representation support usage.
- **Risk & Harm:** severity scoring changes over time, recurrence of similar harms, cumulative impact counts.
- **System Integrity:** missing record flags, data quality errors, audit trail completeness.

## Reporting Obligations (internal, supervisory, public transparency)
- **Internal Reporting:** monthly operational dashboards for management and compliance; exception reporting for SLA breaches and high-severity cases.
- **Supervisory Reporting:** periodic summaries to oversight bodies or boards, including trend analysis and unresolved high-risk cases.
- **Public Transparency:** aggregated, anonymized disclosures on volumes, outcomes, and remedy types, with explanations of safeguards and limitations.

## Institutional Memory & Record Retention
- **Retention:** secure, tamper-evident storage of case records, correspondence, decisions, and evidence bundles.
- **Linkage:** persistent identifiers connecting decisions, escalations, appeals, and external referrals across A1–A4.
- **Continuity:** handover protocols ensuring personnel changes do not disrupt case continuity or accountability.
- **Archiving:** retention schedules aligned with legal obligations and risk-based considerations.

## Periodic Review & Feedback Loops
- **Review Cadence:** quarterly reviews of metrics and systemic issues; annual policy recalibration.
- **Root-Cause Analysis:** post-mortems on SLA breaches, repeated harms, and external referrals.
- **Policy Updates:** updates to procedures, templates, and decision aids based on findings.
- **Training Feedback:** targeted training adjustments for recurring deficiencies.

## Safeguards (privacy, proportionality, access control)
- **Privacy:** data minimization, purpose limitation, and secure handling of sensitive records.
- **Proportionality:** calibrated monitoring based on risk and materiality; avoid excessive surveillance.
- **Access Control:** role-based access, audit logs of access, and separation of duties.
- **Security:** encryption at rest and in transit; incident response for breaches.

## Outputs & Audit Trail
- **Monitoring Dashboard:** real-time metrics and SLA compliance status.
- **Reporting Pack:** monthly/quarterly reports with trends, exceptions, and corrective actions.
- **Institutional Memory Register:** linked case history with decision rationale and remedy outcomes.
- **Audit Logs:** immutable record of changes, access events, and disclosure history.

## Use Cases
- Tracking a surge in escalations tied to a new policy rollout.
- Identifying systemic delays in appeals processing and triggering remediation.
- Supporting external oversight inquiries with a consolidated evidence trail.
- Publishing annual transparency reports on remedy outcomes and compliance trends.

## Compliance & Risk Positioning
- Demonstrates proactive compliance with transparency and effective remedy obligations.
- Reduces regulatory and litigation risk through consistent monitoring and documented accountability.
- Enhances trust by preserving institutional memory and supporting public transparency.
