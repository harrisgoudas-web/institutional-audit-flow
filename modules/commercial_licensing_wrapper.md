# Commercial & Licensing Wrapper (Trigger Project)

## Module Purpose
Define a productized, market-ready specification that packages the Trigger Project compliance framework for institutional buyers. This wrapper translates internal governance modules into a licensable, audit-safe compliance product suitable for banks, tax authorities, regulators, and large organizations without asserting legal authority or jurisdiction.

## Product Description (What It Is / What It Is Not)
**What it is**
- A structured, documentation-first compliance framework that organizes governance controls, audit checkpoints, and evidence bundles for institutional review.
- A modular toolkit that aligns existing legal-institutional components (oversight, remedy, escalation, monitoring) into a deployable compliance program.
- An external-facing specification that supports audit readiness, risk reduction, and regulator-facing transparency.

**What it is not**
- Not a regulator, authority, or legal decision-maker.
- Not a substitute for legal counsel or statutory compliance obligations.
- Not a determination of rights, liabilities, or jurisdictional standing.

## Target Customers & Institutional Buyers
- **Banks & financial institutions**: Chief Compliance Officers, AML/financial crime teams, risk committees, internal audit.
- **Tax authorities & revenue agencies**: Enforcement governance, appeals handling, administrative review units.
- **Regulators & supervisory bodies**: Market conduct, licensing oversight, enforcement coordination teams.
- **Large enterprises & platforms**: General counsel, governance & risk, trust & safety, procurement risk.
- **Public sector entities**: Ombudsman offices, procurement governance, delegated service oversight.

## Commercial Use Cases
- **Banking**: Documented decision rationale, escalation pathways, and audit evidence for account restrictions, sanctions screening, or de-risking actions.
- **Tax & revenue**: Structured remedy and appeals pathways, documentation of decision timeliness, and audit trail of notices.
- **AML & financial crime**: Evidence-ready monitoring and escalation workflow with defensible reason codes.
- **Public sector**: Oversight of delegated service providers, complaint handling transparency, and record retention for public accountability.
- **Regulatory programs**: External-facing audit toolkit to validate institutional controls without asserting legal authority.

## Licensing & Pricing Logic (Indicative, Non-Binding)
- **Annual enterprise license**: Organization-wide usage, documentation updates, and internal training materials.
  - Indicative range: USD 50k–250k per year based on institution size and complexity.
- **Per-entity license**: Pricing aligned to legal entities, subsidiaries, or regulated units.
  - Indicative range: USD 10k–75k per entity per year depending on scope.
- **Per-audit license**: Time-bound, audit-specific deployment for limited engagements.
  - Indicative range: USD 5k–30k per audit engagement.
- **Add-on advisory services**: Optional workshops, configuration support, and documentation alignment services.
  - Indicative range: USD 15k–100k depending on depth and duration.

*Pricing ranges are illustrative only and do not constitute an offer, quote, or commitment.*

## Deployment Formats
- **Documentation pack**: Policy templates, control checklists, evidence bundle formats, and compliance narrative guidance.
- **Advisory module**: Implementation guidance for mapping existing controls to the framework.
- **Audit toolkit**: Pre-built audit checkpoints, logs, and regulator-friendly reporting formats.
- **Training & enablement**: Optional onboarding materials for compliance, audit, and legal teams.

## Value Proposition
- **Risk reduction**: Standardized controls and escalation paths reduce compliance and reputational risk.
- **Audit readiness**: Evidence-centric artifacts enable timely responses to regulator or auditor inquiries.
- **Operational clarity**: Defined triggers, responsibilities, and remediation workflows support consistent execution.
- **Reputational shielding**: Transparent, documented processes help demonstrate due diligence and procedural fairness.

## Disclaimer & Legal Safety Positioning
- This module provides a non-binding compliance specification for internal governance and audit preparation.
- It does not confer legal authority, adjudicate rights, or assert jurisdiction.
- Use of this framework does not replace statutory obligations or legal counsel.
- All outputs should be reviewed and adapted to local legal requirements and institutional policies.

## Compliance & Regulator-Friendly Positioning
This wrapper is designed as an audit-safe, regulator-friendly artifact that supports oversight, transparency, and procedural rigor. It is intended for external sharing as a compliance aid, without implying regulatory status or legal endorsement.
