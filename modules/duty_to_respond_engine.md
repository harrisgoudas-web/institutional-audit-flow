# Duty-to-Respond & Escalation Engine

## Purpose
Define mandatory response obligations, time-bound escalation paths, and accountability mechanisms triggered by detected institutional silence.

## Legal Basis
- **ICCPR**: Articles 2(3) (effective remedy), 14 (fair trial).
- **ECHR**: Articles 6 (fair hearing), 13 (effective remedy).

## Scope
Applies to institutions, agencies, or authorities responsible for responding to claims, filings, or notices that create legal obligations to acknowledge, decide, or remediate. Covers both initial non-response and continued failure after reminders.

## Trigger Inputs (from Institutional Silence Detector)
- Unanswered formal notice or filing past statutory or policy deadline.
- Multiple unanswered reminders or follow-up submissions.
- Lapsed decision window despite proof of receipt.
- Pattern of silence across comparable cases indicating systemic failure.

## Escalation Levels
1. **Level 1: Mandatory Acknowledgment**
   - Require written acknowledgment with a response timeline.
   - Set a short cure window and log accountability owner.
2. **Level 2: Supervisory Review**
   - Escalate to supervisory authority or ombuds function.
   - Require documented justification for delay.
3. **Level 3: External Remedy Activation**
   - Trigger external oversight, court filing, or statutory appeal route.
   - Compile a silence dossier with timestamps and evidence.
4. **Level 4: Public Accountability**
   - Prepare disclosure-ready record for public reporting or parliamentary review.
   - Initiate systemic audit or compliance investigation.

## Outputs
- Formal response obligation notice with deadlines.
- Escalation record with responsible parties and evidence.
- Silence dossier suitable for judicial or oversight submission.
- Compliance status marker (resolved, pending, breached).

## Use Cases
- Administrative agencies failing to respond to benefit or asylum applications.
- Courts or tribunals delaying acknowledgement or scheduling beyond statutory limits.
- Regulatory bodies ignoring complaints or enforcement requests.
- Public universities or hospitals failing to answer rights-based petitions.

## Compliance Positioning
Aligns escalation and remedy obligations with ICCPR and ECHR requirements for effective remedy and fair process. Documents institutional silence as a rights-impacting failure, supporting proportional escalation and evidentiary integrity for review or litigation.
