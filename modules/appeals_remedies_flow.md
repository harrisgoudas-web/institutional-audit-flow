# Appeals & Remedies Flow

## Purpose
Define a structured, independent appeals and remedies process following escalation under the Duty-to-Respond & Escalation Engine. This module ensures individuals and impacted parties have access to timely, fair review and effective remedies when institutions fail to respond or issue adverse decisions with material effects.

## Legal Basis
- **ICCPR** Articles 2(3) and 14 (effective remedy and fair hearing).
- **ECHR** Articles 6 and 13 (fair trial and effective remedy).

## Scope & Standing
- **Scope:** Appeals triggered by escalated institutional silence or adverse decisions with material effects on rights, obligations, or legitimate interests.
- **Standing:** Impacted individuals, their authorized representatives, and affected collective entities with a direct and material interest.
- **Exclusions:** Trivial disputes, purely discretionary policy preferences without rights implications, or matters already resolved with no ongoing effects.

## Trigger Inputs (from Duty-to-Respond & Escalation Engine)
- Escalation output confirming non-response beyond mandated timelines.
- Adverse decision notice with material effects.
- Record of prior correspondence, deadlines, and escalation steps.
- Risk categorization (severity, urgency, rights impact).
- Evidence bundle (documents, communications, logs).

## Appeals Levels
1. **Internal Review**
   - Conducted by a separate unit from the original decision-maker.
   - Target outcome: rapid correction or confirmation with written reasons.
2. **Independent Review**
   - Conducted by an internal-but-independent panel or external ombud function.
   - Focus on impartial reassessment of facts, procedure, and legal compliance.
3. **External Remedy**
   - Referral or guidance for external adjudication, regulator review, or judicial remedy.
   - Includes formal escalation dossier and support for access to external fora.

## Procedural Guarantees
- **Right to be Heard:** Opportunity to submit evidence and arguments at each level.
- **Reason-Giving:** Written decisions with factual findings and legal reasoning.
- **Time Limits:** Fixed timelines for receipt, acknowledgment, review, and decision.
- **Access to Records:** Access to relevant non-privileged records and rationale.
- **Non-Retaliation:** Protection against adverse treatment for filing an appeal.

## Remedies & Outcomes
- **Annulment:** Void an invalid or unlawful decision.
- **Correction:** Amend decision outcomes or factual findings.
- **Restitution:** Restore status or benefits where possible.
- **Referral:** Escalate for external enforcement or judicial remedy.
- **Interim Measures:** Temporary relief where ongoing harm is likely.

## Independence & Conflict-of-Interest Safeguards
- Separation of functions between original decision-makers and reviewers.
- Mandatory disclosure and recusal for conflicts of interest.
- Independent panel composition with relevant expertise.
- Auditability of reviewer selection and decision rationale.

## Outputs & Audit Trail
- Appeal intake record with timestamp, trigger source, and scope.
- Decision log including outcomes, reasoning, and remedies.
- Evidence index and access log.
- SLA compliance metrics and breach flags.
- Escalation dossier for external remedy if applicable.

## Use Cases
- Institutional silence on a statutory request leading to rights impact.
- Adverse regulatory or administrative decision with material effects.
- Denial of services or benefits without adequate reasoning.
- Failure to implement a mandated corrective action after escalation.

## Compliance Positioning
- Aligns with due process and effective remedy obligations under ICCPR and ECHR.
- Provides structured, auditable, and rights-respecting escalation pathways.
- Supports institutional accountability through documented review and remedy.
