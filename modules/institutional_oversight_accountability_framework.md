# Institutional Oversight & Accountability Framework

## Module Purpose
Document a compliance-by-design framework that maps de facto power structures (public, private, or hybrid) to oversight and accountability mechanisms aligned with ICCPR and ECHR obligations. The module provides a non-binding, audit-safe specification that supports internal governance, supervisory review, and regulator-ready evidence preparation without asserting authority or legal determinations.

## Legal Basis (Non-Exhaustive)
- **ICCPR**: Articles 2 (effective remedy), 14 (fair hearing), 17 (privacy), 19 (access to information), 21 (assembly), 22 (association), and 26 (non-discrimination) inform the requirements for transparent, reasoned, and accessible oversight.
- **ECHR**: Articles 6 (fair trial), 8 (private life), 10 (expression and access to information), 11 (assembly and association), 13 (effective remedy), and 14 (non-discrimination) establish expectations for accountability and procedural safeguards.

## Definitions
- **Oversight**: A structured, documented review process that verifies whether an entity with de facto authority complies with applicable rights-based obligations and procedural safeguards.
- **Accountability**: The obligation to provide reasoned explanations, remediate harms, and enable effective remedies through documented, reviewable processes.
- **De Facto Authority**: Any entity that exercises practical control over rights-relevant outcomes, regardless of formal legal status, including contractors, platforms, delegated administrators, or private actors performing public functions.

## Oversight Triggers & Thresholds
- **Delegated authority trigger**: Evidence that a private or hybrid actor performs functions that affect protected rights or access to remedies.
- **Systemic impact trigger**: Patterns indicating repeated adverse outcomes (e.g., denial of services, censorship, exclusion) affecting protected classes or rights.
- **Silence trigger**: Failure to provide a timely, reasoned response to lawful requests, complaints, or mandatory disclosures.
- **Risk threshold**: Severity scoring that flags high-impact outcomes (e.g., livelihood impact, liberty constraints, discrimination indicators) for supervisory review.
- **Cross-border trigger**: Operations affecting individuals in jurisdictions with ICCPR/ECHR obligations.

## Oversight Controls (ICCPR/ECHR Translation)
- **Reasoned decision control**: Decisions affecting rights include a documented rationale, legal basis, and remedy options.
- **Timeliness control**: Response and review timelines align with statutory and procedural obligations.
- **Access to information control**: Documented mechanisms for data access, correction, and transparency when rights are implicated.
- **Non-discrimination control**: Evidence of proportionality and equal treatment, including bias monitoring where automated or scaled systems are used.
- **Remedy control**: Accessible appeal or complaint pathways with documented outcomes and escalation thresholds.

## Audit Checkpoints & Evidence Requirements
- **Mapping checkpoint**: Updated registry of de facto authority relationships, contractual delegation, and operational control pathways.
  - Evidence: delegation matrix, role descriptions, service-level obligations, jurisdictional scope.
- **Decision checkpoint**: Verification that decisions are reasoned, reviewable, and traceable to policy/legal bases.
  - Evidence: decision logs, rationale statements, policy references, reviewer sign-off.
- **Access checkpoint**: Proof of access to information and correction mechanisms.
  - Evidence: request logs, response timelines, data access records, correction outcomes.
- **Remedy checkpoint**: Confirmation that remedies and appeal pathways are available and communicated.
  - Evidence: appeal notices, escalation records, remedy outcomes, communications templates.
- **Non-discrimination checkpoint**: Monitoring and documentation of equity impacts.
  - Evidence: impact assessments, bias audits, exception logs, mitigation actions.

## Required Audit Artefacts & Logs
- Oversight register of de facto authority relationships and delegated functions.
- Trigger event log (silence, risk, systemic impact) with timestamps and severity scores.
- Supervisory review queue with reviewer assignments and outcome states.
- Evidence bundle templates for internal audit, counsel review, or regulator inquiries.
- Change log for policy updates, remediation actions, and control improvements.

## Interfaces: Detection to Supervisory Review
- **Detection inputs**: Silence detection, risk classification, and systemic impact indicators feed into a supervisory review queue.
- **Review workflow**: Triage, assign reviewer, document findings, determine remediation or escalation.
- **Escalation paths**: Internal audit, legal counsel, senior governance committee, or regulator engagement where required.
- **Closure criteria**: Documented remediation, user notification, and verification of control effectiveness.

## Use Cases
- **Regulators**: Receive structured evidence bundles demonstrating oversight of de facto authority and remedy pathways.
- **Banks & financial institutions**: Monitor outsourced or platform-mediated decisions that affect account access, sanctions, or service denial.
- **Public bodies**: Track delegated service providers and ensure transparency and remedy availability for affected individuals.

## Compliance & Commercial Positioning
This module is a documentation-only, compliance-by-design framework. It supports audit readiness and governance transparency without asserting regulatory authority or legal conclusions. The specification can be offered as a neutral, externally shareable artefact that helps organizations demonstrate alignment with ICCPR/ECHR-aligned oversight expectations while preserving internal discretion and jurisdictional applicability.
