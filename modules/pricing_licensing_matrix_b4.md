# B4 – Pricing & Licensing Matrix (Trigger Compliance Framework)

## Module Purpose
Define a non-binding, audit-safe pricing and licensing structure that translates existing Trigger compliance modules into institutional products. This specification is designed for external sharing with banks, regulators, tax authorities, and public bodies without asserting legal authority, jurisdiction, or binding effect.

## Scope
- Establish indicative pricing tiers aligned with institutional risk, size, and deployment scope.
- Define licensing models for annual access, institutional licensing, and strategic deployments.
- Clarify tier inclusions for documentation, tooling, and advisory layers.
- Ensure compatibility with public procurement, regulatory review, and internal audit requirements.

## Pricing Principles
- **Proportionality**: Pricing scales with risk exposure, organizational size, and deployment scope.
- **Transparency**: Clear inclusions, defined exclusions, and no hidden obligations.
- **Non-exclusivity**: No exclusivity commitments are implied or required.
- **Compliance-by-design monetization**: Fees reflect documentation, tooling, and governance support; not enforcement or legal authority.

## Pricing Tiers (Indicative Annual Ranges)
*All ranges are illustrative, non-binding, and subject to procurement processes and internal approvals.*

| Tier | Indicative Annual Range (USD) | Target Buyer Profile | Typical Deployment Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Tier Inclusions (Documentation, Toolkit, Advisory Layers)
- **Tier 1**
  - Core documentation pack (policy templates, audit checklists, evidence formats).
  - Baseline governance mapping and module index.
  - Standard update notices (annual release cadence).
- **Tier 2**
  - Tier 1 inclusions.
  - Expanded toolkit (audit checkpoints, log formats, escalation templates).
  - Implementation guidance for integrating existing controls.
- **Tier 3**
  - Tier 2 inclusions.
  - Advanced governance mapping and cross-unit oversight alignment guidance.
  - Optional advisory sessions for procurement-ready documentation alignment.
- **Tier 4**
  - Tier 3 inclusions.
  - Strategic deployment support for multi-entity or regulator-facing programs.
  - Enhanced audit packaging for regulator or public sector review.

## Licensing Models
- **Annual Access License**
  - Non-exclusive, non-transferable access to documentation and updates for a defined institution or unit.
  - Updates provided on a scheduled release basis; no implied legal or regulatory endorsement.
- **Institutional License**
  - Organization-wide access for a defined legal entity and its named subsidiaries.
  - Non-transferable; sublicensing only if explicitly documented in procurement terms.
- **Strategic Deployment License**
  - Expanded access for multi-entity or regulator-facing deployments.
  - Includes audit packaging and structured evidence bundle formats aligned to public oversight needs.

## Access Rights & Update Policy
- Access is limited to the purchasing institution and specified authorized teams.
- Updates are provided as documentation releases and do not imply legal changes or binding standards.
- Use of the framework is at the institution’s discretion and must be adapted to local law and policy.

## Procurement, Audit, and Regulatory Compatibility
- Designed as an attachment-ready, regulator-friendly specification for procurement review.
- Clear documentation boundaries to support internal audit review and evidence management.
- No exclusivity requirements; compatible with competitive procurement and multi-vendor compliance strategies.

## Audit-Safe Positioning Language (External Sharing)
This matrix provides indicative pricing and licensing logic for a documentation-first compliance framework. It is intended to support institutional procurement, audit readiness, and regulator-facing transparency without asserting legal authority, jurisdiction, or binding effect. Pricing reflects documentation, tooling, and governance support only.
