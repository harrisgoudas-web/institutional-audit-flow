# Institutional Silence Detector

## Purpose
Define institutional silence as a legally relevant event under the ICCPR and ECHR, enabling auditable detection and response workflows for regulated entities and public bodies.

## Legal Basis
- **ICCPR**: Articles 2 (effective remedies), 14 (fair hearing), 19 (freedom of expression and access to information), and 26 (non-discrimination) support the requirement for timely, reasoned responses by public authorities.
- **ECHR**: Articles 6 (fair trial), 10 (freedom of expression), and 13 (effective remedy) establish expectations for prompt, reasoned decisions and accessible remedies when authorities fail to act.

## Definition of Institutional Silence
Institutional silence is the failure of a public authority, regulated entity, or delegated administrative body to provide a timely, reasoned response to a lawful request, complaint, or mandatory disclosure obligation where a response is legally required.

## Trigger Thresholds
- **Statutory deadline breach**: No response issued within the legally prescribed timeframe.
- **Reasoned decision failure**: Response issued but lacks required legal reasoning, findings, or references.
- **Procedural acknowledgment lapse**: No acknowledgment of receipt within required administrative timelines.
- **Remedy access delay**: Failure to communicate appeal or remedy options within mandated periods.

## Outputs
- **Silence event log**: Timestamped record of missed deadlines and non-responses.
- **Legal risk classification**: Severity scoring mapped to ICCPR/ECHR obligations.
- **Escalation packets**: Evidence bundles for internal audit, counsel review, or regulator notification.
- **Compliance dashboard flags**: Real-time indicators for unresolved silence events.

## Use Cases
- Oversight of public authority response obligations to petitions or FOI requests.
- Monitoring regulated entity complaint handling timelines.
- Tracking administrative decision delays affecting protected rights.
- Supporting litigation readiness for denial of effective remedy.

## Commercial Positioning
A standalone compliance module that monetizes institutional accountability by transforming silence into a measurable legal event, enabling premium reporting, escalation services, and regulator-ready evidence packages for enterprises and public sector clients.
